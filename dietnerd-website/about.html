About ChronicNerd

Research context for
better health conversations.

ChronicNerd is the conversational DietNerd project in the CustomNerd research family. It provides evidence-based diet and nutrition answers while preserving durable conversations, rolling summaries, follow-up context, and user document uploads.

Evidencemade clear
Research companion, not medical advice. DietNerd is an exploratory tool designed to enrich conversations with a registered dietitian or registered dietitian nutritionist. It may not account for every medication interaction or pre-existing condition. Find a nutrition expert.
How it works

From a question to published evidence.

DietNerd searches peer-reviewed research from PubMed and presents a clear answer alongside article-level analysis. Live progress makes the research path visible, while source summaries help people understand each study and open the original paper for more context.
